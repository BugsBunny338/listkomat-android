A public-transport ticket in one text message. No sign-up, no credit top-ups, no internet needed.

Lístkomat remembers the phone numbers and SMS codes for transit tickets in 10 Czech cities. Pick a ticket, the app prefills the SMS, you just send it — the ticket arrives right back and the price lands on your operator's bill.

WHY LÍSTKOMAT
• No registration, no account — open it and buy
• No credit top-ups — billed by your mobile operator
• Works without internet — an SMS signal is enough
• 10 cities in one app: Prague, Brno, Ostrava, Plzeň, Liberec, Olomouc, Ústí nad Labem, Hradec Králové, České Budějovice and Pardubice

SMART TOUCHES
• Location-aware: the app offers tickets for the city you're in (your location never leaves the device)
• Ticket-expiry notification
• Live transit map for Prague and Brno — trams, buses and trolleybuses in real time
• Optional visual themes

GOOD TO KNOW
• SMS tickets require a Czech SIM card (premium SMS billed by your operator)
• Price lists update automatically; the last known prices work offline too
• No analytics, no tracking — the app collects no data
